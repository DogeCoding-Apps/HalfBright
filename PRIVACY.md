# Privacy Policy for HalfBright

**Last updated:** November 2026

**HalfBright** does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app runs completely offline, and your settings and dimming profiles stay on your computer under your control.

Because HalfBright is distributed through the Microsoft Store, Windows and the Microsoft Store may collect standard diagnostic information according to your system settings and Microsoft’s privacy policies.

### Contact
If you have any questions, reach out at **DogeCoding@outlook.com** or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/DogeCoding-Apps/HalfBright/issues).
